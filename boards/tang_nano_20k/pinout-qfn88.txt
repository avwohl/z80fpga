Package pins of the GW2AR-LV18QN88C8/I7, recovered from apicula's own chip
database (GW2A-18C.msgpack.xz, the pinout/QFN88 table) rather than from a
datasheet, so it agrees with what nextpnr will actually accept in a .cst.

The right-hand column is what this board is known to wire each pin to, from
tang_nano_20k.cst and the board notes.  A blank there does NOT mean the pin
is free: it means nothing in this repository uses it, and the Sipeed
schematic has to say what the board does with it before anything drives it.
Several of these go to the HDMI connector.  The GW2AR's SDRAM is bonded
inside the package and does not appear here at all.

Why it is in the tree: with this board's console dead, every route to getting
output off it -- a second UART on a header pin, or a text console on HDMI --
starts with knowing which pin is which, and that was the slow part every
time.  The configuration-SPI pins in particular are the flash readback
channel described in the README.
