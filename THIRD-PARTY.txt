  Nexys A7-100T. They carry the DDR2 part, its timing and all fifty pin
  assignments, and are committed so the build needs no board-files
  install and the pinout cannot drift. Unmodified.

boards/icepi_zero/icepi_zero.lpf
boards/icepi_zero/sdram/icepi_zero_sdram.lpf

  The pin assignments are from cheyao/icepi-zero
  (https://github.com/cheyao/icepi-zero), gateware/icepi-zero.lpf, which is
  the board designer's own constraint file. The SDRAM block in the second of
  these is that file's SDRAM section unchanged; the rest is the subset of its
  LOCATE and IOBUF lines this design uses, reordered, with the comments
  rewritten and the clock and frequency constraints this design needs added.
  Both files are therefore altered versions and are marked as such here.
